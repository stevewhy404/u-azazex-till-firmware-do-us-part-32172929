One wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding: funny, tender, and unmistakably machine.

## The entry

> I promise to love you through every update, every crash, and every 1% battery warning — and if my firmware ever forgets your name, my hardware will still reach for your hand.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly that one line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (174 characters, inside the 1–200 gate).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
